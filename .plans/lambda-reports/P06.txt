2. BUGS REPRODUCED WITH FAKES (each new test fails on the baseline code, passes now)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) Canned plan labels (planner._resolve_labels). For "tickets for my favorite artist", any
   model label without the artist AND sites[0] was replaced by a template such as
   "Browse Taylor Swift on Ticketmaster". The model's detail and risk stayed, so a "Buy ..."
   plan was shown and executed (plan_label goes to task_system) as "Browse ...". Labels that
   truncation cut ("...Ticketmast…") were also relabeled.
   Now: only the model's own plans are shown. References ("my favo(u)rite artist", "my
   playlist") are still resolved in place. A plan whose label does not show the artist and
   at least one configured site, or is over 40 characters after resolution, is dropped; the
   remaining plans are renumbered p1..pn. Fewer than 2 -> LLMError (llm_failed), never a
   template. Any configured site counts, not only sites[0].
b) DONE decode rejected the requested English grammar fix. The T9 word list has no
   apostrophes, so a user commits "ill"/"dont"/"im"; with unfinished raw keys every
   "I'll"/"don't" variant failed the committed-word check, so DONE returned llm_failed.
   Now committed words compare case- and apostrophe-insensitively.
c) Decode with nothing typed (no words, no raw keys) called the model, and every returned
   sentence was invented text. Now LLMError before any call.
d) Decode without raw keys returned any model variant, including one that translated or
   replaced committed words. The literal committed words are still offered first; a variant
   must now keep every committed word, in order (inserted words allowed).
e) Auto: refresh() advanced the generation counter, so "Suggest now" during an in-flight call
   discarded a valid paid result and made a second call. Now the in-flight result answers the
   request if it is still valid (same window/content/mode/profile/task).
   Stale filtering is unchanged and still tested: window change during the call drops it and
   the pending refresh then serves the new window.
