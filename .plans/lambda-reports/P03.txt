   fails before clipboard access and invalidates old IDs. Focus changing during
   implicit target lookup is also rejected before writing AXValue to a new field.
2. A window/focus switch during clipboard copying or writing could redirect
   paste; per-character fallback could continue in the new context. Pin native
   window/focus and recheck after preparation, before paste, after paste wait,
   and before each fallback character. Restore all saved clipboard items/types
   on cancellation/error paths after a clipboard write.
3. type_text(submit=True) could press Enter after typing changed native context.
   Recheck the supplied snapshot and STOP before the explicit submit. This is
   conservative: intentional focus/title changes require a fresh read before a
   later submit. The STOP-after-type test also enforces a dispatch-level check;
   the existing real press_keys already had its own pre-key cancellation guard.
4. Failed AXPress followed by a same-title window switch could redirect its
   coordinate fallback. Verify the snapshot again immediately before the
   fallback event. A cancelled direct click now stops before native resolution.
5. STOP during Quartz key-event construction still allowed that key-down to be
   posted. Recheck after construction/flags, before recording/posting held keys;
   preserve reverse releases and remaining modifier flags.
6. A child exiting between poll and kill raised ProcessLookupError, masked the
   intended timeout/cancellation and skipped wait. Tolerate that exit race and

+            state.exit_at = 0

+                       match="task stopped" if stopped else "application_activation_timeout"):
